# Prompts para o Google Flow (Veo) — "O Relojoeiro"

## Como usar
1. Flow → projeto **vertical 9:16** → **imagem para vídeo** (a imagem é o primeiro quadro) → modelo **Fast** para testar e **Quality** só nos planos finais.
2. Cole o **BLOCO FIXO** e logo depois a linha do plano. Sem imagem? Use o prompt completo da seção "Só texto" em `roteiro.md` (coluna Prompt) no lugar do movimento.
3. Gere 2 a 3 tomadas, baixe a melhor e salve como `p01.mp4`, `p02.mp4` ... em `montagem/clips/`.
4. Imagens que já existem: planos 1, 14, 24 e 29 (`montagem/clips/p01.jpg` etc.). Para os outros, gere a imagem do plano (prompt do `roteiro.md`) ou use o último quadro do plano anterior como primeiro quadro.

## BLOCO FIXO (cole no começo de todo prompt)
```
3D animated feature film, Pixar-like, soft subsurface skin, expressive eyes, cinematic shallow depth of field, volumetric light. Characters stay identical to the reference image: LIA (curly auburn hair, freckles, mustard-yellow wool scarf) and THE CLOCKMAKER (tall, silver hair, round gold spectacles, long dark-teal coat with brass buttons). Prop: a brass pocket watch with a tiny amber glowing light. No text, no subtitles, no logos on screen. No music, no speech; only subtle ambient sound (rain, soft clock ticking). Smooth, slow, natural movement.
```

## Planos (duração alvo entre parênteses; gere um pouco mais longo, o script corta)

**Ato 1 — gancho e regra**
1. (4s) Slow push-in on the rainy street. Rain falls, the lamppost light flickers softly, the Clockmaker holds out the glowing watch and the little girl looks up at him. Amber glow pulses gently.
2. (3s) Extreme close-up of the pocket watch swinging slowly on its chain, amber light reflecting on wet cobblestones, raindrops falling in the foreground.
3. (3s) The Clockmaker slowly kneels down to the girl's height and offers the open watch; she watches with wide curious eyes. Camera drifts slightly to the side.
4. (4s) Close-up of his hands opening the watch; tiny amber sparks float upward like fireflies; a faint chime.
5. (3s) Close-up of the girl's small hand reaching out and touching the watch; amber light grows on her face.
6. (3s) Insert shot: the watch's single hand turns slowly, then stops; a soft amber pulse.
7. (3s) The girl clutches the watch to her chest and nods with determination; slow push-in on her face.

**Ato 2 — escalada (cada presente: causa → milagre; as faíscas âmbar aparecem só na doação)**
8. (3s) A father in a wet coat runs toward the school auditorium door; the girl peeks from behind the stage curtain. Handheld feel, urgency.
9. (3s) A soft amber spark glows from the girl's pocket; the curtain pauses, the father sits down breathless and relieved as the curtain begins to rise; the girl smiles.
10. (3s) Teen Lia at a bus stop in evening rain; her best friend with a suitcase turns toward the bus. Slow lateral move.
11. (3s) Amber light blooms from her coat pocket; the bus waits with doors open; the two friends hug tightly. Slow push-in.
12. (3s) Wide shot of a busy train station; a small boy cries in the crowd; young Lia notices him and turns. Crowd moves around.
13. (3s) Amber glow; the boy runs into his mother's arms; far in the background the Clockmaker watches with a small smile.
14. (4s) Wedding altar; the groom's hands tremble, the bride touches the watch in her bouquet, amber glow spreads; he exhales and begins to speak, smiling. Slow push-in.
15. (3s) Warm kitchen at night; Lia grabs the watch from the counter as her teenage son rushes with his backpack; amber spark.
16. (3s) The boy catches the school bus as its doors stay open for him; amber light on the wet road.
17. (4s) Extreme close-up of the Clockmaker's eyes reflecting a dimming amber glow; his brow furrows with worry; he looks away.
18. (3s) Lia, older, and an elderly neighbor on a porch; the golden sunset glows brighter and seems to hold still; warm amber light from the watch.
19. (3s) Close-up of the elderly neighbor's face lit by the golden sky; happy tears.
20. (3s) Old Lia writes letters at a desk by lamplight; the watch beside her is almost dark; slow push-in.
21. (3s) Through a rainy window: the Clockmaker outside hesitates to come in; warm light inside, cold rain outside.

**Ato 3 — virada (movimento mínimo, silêncio)**
22. (4s) A hospital room at night, cold teal light; 85-year-old Lia in an armchair with a white scarf; the Clockmaker enters slowly and removes his hat.
23. (4s) Extreme close-up of the Clockmaker's face; his eyes fill with tears; he lowers his gaze. Almost still, slow push-in.
24. (4s) Extreme close-up of old Lia; a calm knowing smile slowly forms; warm lamplight on one side of her face.
25. (4s) Close-up of the Clockmaker's hand holding the pocket watch, completely dark; his fingers tremble slightly.
26. (3s) Close-up of Lia's wrinkled hand reaching and holding his hand over the dark watch.

**Ato 4 — eco (a luz volta)**
27. (4s) The hospital door opens; a group of people of different ages enters, each holding a tiny glowing amber pocket watch; golden light floods the cold room. Slow push-in.
28. (4s) Everyone opens their watch; amber embers float through the air toward Lia's white scarf, which slowly turns mustard-yellow again; joyful faces.
29. (4s) Old Lia offers a tiny glowing amber spark in her open palm to the Clockmaker; he smiles for the first time with misty eyes; embers float around them.
30. (4s) Wide shot of the hospital window at sunrise; warm golden light; silhouettes holding hands; the watch ticks softly on the table. Slow pull-back; fade-friendly ending.
